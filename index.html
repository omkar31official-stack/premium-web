Our Story

A Legacy of Passion

1998

Born from Fire, Perfected by Time

In a hidden corner of the city, Chef Alessandro Noir first struck a match and ignited more than a flame — he ignited a philosophy. The belief that great dining is not merely about sustenance, but about transcendence.

For over two decades, Noir & Flame has stood as a testament to uncompromising quality, where every ingredient is sourced with intention and every dish is composed like a symphony.

— Chef Alessandro Noir, Founder
